Accuracy, and why you should still check

Tally reads bank alerts and marketplace emails automatically, and the categories it suggests are guesses learned from your own past choices. Automatic capture can miss a transaction, read one twice, misread an amount, or file something under the wrong category. Currency conversions are labelled estimates and are not the rate your bank actually applied. Your bank's statement is the authority, not Tally. Do not rely on Tally for tax, accounting, legal or investment decisions, and check anything that matters against the source.
