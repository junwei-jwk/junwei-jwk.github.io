Tally
A personal expense tracker

Know where the money went.

Tally is an Android app that records what you spend. You add a purchase in a couple of taps, and it can also read your bank's own transaction alerts so most of them are already there when you look.

What it does

Records it offline

Every entry is written to the phone first and returns immediately. Losing signal is a normal state, not an error — your data syncs when the network comes back.

Reads the bank's alert

With your permission, Tally reads transaction alerts from a fixed list of bank senders and turns each one into an entry for you to confirm.

Learns your categories

The category it suggests comes from what you have picked for that merchant before. A suggestion is always a suggestion — nothing is filed without you.

Counts in cents

Amounts are stored as whole cents, never as floating point, so a total is the sum of its parts down to the last cent.

Handles foreign spend

An overseas charge keeps both the original currency and a clearly labelled estimate in SGD, alongside any cashback the card earned.

Budgets by envelope

Allocate a month's money across envelopes, roll the remainder forward, and get a warning before a category runs dry rather than after.

Who it is for

Tally is built and run by one person for their own use, and shared with a small number of invited testers. It is not sold, it carries no advertising, and it has nothing to upsell you. If you are testing it, the terms of service and the privacy policy apply to you too.

About your data

Tally does not sell, rent or share your data, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train any machine-learning model. If you turn on email capture, it asks Google for read-only access to your Gmail and requests only mail from a fixed list of bank and payment-provider domains. It never stores the subject or body of an email. The privacy policy lists every domain and says exactly what is kept.
